PRIVACY POLICY

1. Introduction

Welcome to VATROC website. We take your privacy and personal data security seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website, participate in air traffic control (ATC) training, events, or related services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide smooth virtual air traffic and pilot support services, we may collect the following information:

  • VATSIM Account Details: Your VATSIM CID, full name, registered email address, and home division.
  • ATC & Flight Records: Connection hours, controller ratings, event participation history, and flight data.
  • Form Submissions: Contact details and personal backgrounds provided when submitting training applications, support forms, or inquiries.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Reviewing and managing controller training, examinations, and ratings within the Taipei FIR (VATROC).
  • Organizing and notifying participants regarding online/offline ATC and pilot events.
  • Maintaining website stability, optimizing user experiences, and preventing platform abuse.

4. Data Protection and Third-Party Sharing

We implement appropriate security measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Except as required by VATSIM regulations, necessary data exchange with partner divisions (e.g., VATPRC, VATKOR), or compliance with legal obligations, we will never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to unrelated third parties.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect regulatory changes or division requirements. Any revisions will be posted directly on this page. If you have any questions regarding this policy, please feel free to reach out to our management team via our official Discord server.
